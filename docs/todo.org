** TODO Boot timing on the test card
Test-card boots 1-3 handed over to Debian at 8.1-9.2 s, boot 4 at
4.84 s, the sway card always at ~4.9 s. The extra time is in stage 1's
writes to P25TBOOT (each followed by =sync=), not in the initramfs
(boots 3 and 4: same initramfs, identical kernel logs). The FAT format
is the same as card 1's (512-byte clusters). Likely cause: the new card
(SanDisk A1 128 GB) is slow on small writes for a while after the first
large write.

  =tar czf ~/testcard-logs.tar.gz -C /boot/firmware logs=, copy to
  =~/p25t-backup/=; compare "switching to Debian at" in
  =boot-N-handover.txt=.
- If it stays slow: test the card's write speed on the PC.
** WAIT USB2 PHY tuning (not tested)
The bring-up's =initramfs/init= has a USB2 PHY tuning block (stock
kernel values: pre-emphasis, eye height, bvalid filter; =devmem= writes
to the USB PHY and GRF, not to the RK817). It was added after the cards'
initramfs was built, so it has never run on a card, although the
bring-up docs say it does. It may help the flaky USB gadget.
- Kept out of =initramfs/init= here until tested on its own.
- Test: add it to the test card's init, compare gadget enumeration over
  several boots with and without it (=/boot/no-usb-tuning= skips it).

** DONE Volume-key rescue
CLOSED: [2026-10-21]
Holding a volume key at power-on did not keep the stage-1 system: init
read =iio:device0=, which since the accelerometer (2026-09-26) is the
SC7A20, while the keys' ADC is =iio:device1= (=fe720000.saradc=); every
boot logged "volume key ADC: unreadable". =initramfs/init= now finds
the ADC by its name. Test card, initramfs put on with
=tools/deploy_initramfs.sh=: boot 6 normal (ADC 1023 on iio:device1,
Debian), boot 7 with a volume key held (775, stayed in stage 1), boot 8
normal again.
