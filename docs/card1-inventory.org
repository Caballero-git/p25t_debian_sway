* Decided (Jose, 2026-10-10)
  - Keyboard: Spanish and US on both cards. Console: ~XKBLAYOUT="es,us"~,
    ~grp:alt_shift_toggle~ (Alt+Shift switches). Sway: the same two
    layouts in its own config.
  - Locale: ~LANG=en_US.UTF-8~. en_GB and es_ES are generated too,
    because ssh from the PC passes its own locale (AcceptEnv).
  - syncthing on both cards.
  - Dotfiles are not copied: the new card starts with Debian's defaults.
  - New for both cards (my addition, easy to drop):
    ~/etc/apt/apt.conf.d/90-p25t-no-recommends~, so later
    ~apt install~ on the tablet also skips Recommends (exim4 lesson).
